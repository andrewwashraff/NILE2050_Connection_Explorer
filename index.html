Tourism & Story Paths Shahd Alabbasy 12 connections

Project Description

This project acts as a cultural tourism transition hub that connects Red Sea recreation with Upper Egypt’s heritage corridor. Located near Qena and Dendera, it introduces visitors to Sa‘idi identity through museums, craft workshops, performances, hospitality, dining, and exhibitions. Inspired by weaving, the architecture translates interlaced threads into connected spaces and circulation paths, creating a cultural gateway where tourists and locals exchange traditions, crafts, and experiences.
